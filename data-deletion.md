# Emoji Magic - Data Deletion Requests

**Developer:** Yair Goren Apps  
**Contact:** yairgorenn@gmail.com

## How to Request Data Deletion

Emoji Magic does not require user registration or accounts.

If you would like to request the deletion of data associated with your use of Emoji Magic:

1. Send an email to **yairgorenn@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject **Emoji Magic - Data Deletion Request**.
3. Describe your request. You do not need to provide passwords or sensitive personal information.

We will review your request and respond by email.

## What Data Is Deleted or Retained?

- **Submitted messages:** Emoji Magic does not store your submitted text or AI-generated responses in its application database.
- **Usage statistics:** We store technical information such as request timestamps, text lengths, selected styles and processing times. These records are not linked to individual user accounts or identifiers, so we generally cannot identify and delete a specific user's usage records.
- **Support requests:** Information you send by email may be retained as necessary to handle your request and related correspondence.
- **Third-party processing:** Text sent to OpenAI may be subject to the provider's separate retention and data handling practices.

We do not assign a fixed retention period to anonymous usage statistics at this time. They may be retained for operational monitoring until deleted by the developer.

For more information, see our [Privacy Policy](https://yairgorenn.github.io/).
