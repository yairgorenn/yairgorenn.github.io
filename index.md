# Emoji Magic - Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

**Developer:** Yair Goren Apps  
**Contact:** yairgorenn@gmail.com

### 1. About Emoji Magic

Emoji Magic is an application that uses artificial intelligence to enhance text messages with emojis and alternative wording.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as necessary.

### 2. How Your Text Is Processed

When you use Emoji Magic:

- Your text is securely transmitted to our backend server hosted on Railway.
- The server sends the text to OpenAI for AI processing.
- The generated results are returned to the application.

The application does not store your submitted text or generated results in our database.

Text submitted for processing is shared with OpenAI, which processes it according to its applicable data handling and privacy policies.

### 3. Usage Statistics

We collect limited technical information to monitor application performance and usage, including:

- Number and time of requests.
- Selected emoji and paraphrasing styles.
- Input text length.
- Request success or failure.
- Processing duration.
- AI model used.

These statistics do not include the content of your messages or a user account identifier.

### 4. Accounts and Personal Information

Emoji Magic does not require users to create an account.

We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact lists through the application's text-processing service.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into the application.

### 5. Data Storage and Retention

Usage statistics are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway for operational monitoring and analysis.

Submitted messages and generated responses are not stored in our application database.

Usage statistics may be retained for operational purposes and can be deleted by the developer.

Third-party service providers may process or retain information according to their applicable policies.

### 6. Data Security

We use HTTPS connections to transmit information between the application, our backend server, and the AI service.

We take reasonable precautions to protect application data, although no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

### 7. Data Deletion and Inquiries

Users may contact us regarding privacy questions or data deletion requests at:

**yairgorenn@gmail.com**

Because the application does not require accounts or store user identifiers with usage statistics, we may be unable to associate anonymous usage records with a particular user.

### 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's functionality or data processing practices change.

Updates will be published on this page.
